<p>I'm a big fan of harm reduction. Passkeys are <em>better</em> than passwords. Nuclear power is <em>better</em> than coal or LNG. Vaping is <em>better</em> (maybe?) than smoking.<br><br>Harm reduction isn't perfect. It's often not even good enough. But it's better then the status quo, and that's important.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Yes, moral hazard and risk homeostasis exist. We should always work toward improving things; adopting a better solution isn't an excuse for complacency.<br><br>But better is still better. Don't let the perfect be the enemy of the good. Incremental improvement is real improvement. Embrace it!</p>
