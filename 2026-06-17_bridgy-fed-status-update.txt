Hi everyone! Welcome to another Bridgy Fed status update!

Our biggest focus [since last time](/2026-05-01_56963) was [migration and credible exit](https://blog.anew.social/migrations-update-launch/): letting you move your bridged accounts onto and off of the bridge, in both directions, on every network. We also worked on more [cost cutting](https://blog.anew.social/bridging-on-a-budget/) and other internal improvements.

Here are all the other features and changes we've shipped recently:

* [Preserve whitespace more faithfully](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed/issues/990)
* [Label automated accounts as bots](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed/issues/2513)
* [Support more than four images](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed/issues/2504) in Atmosphere (Bluesky) posts
* [Include aspect ratio with images to the fediverse](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed/issues/2502)
* [Add IndieAuth login to the web UI](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed/issues/2185)
* [When a new user joins, DM everyone who requested them](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed/issues/1321)
* [Include account links](https://blog.anew.social/bridging-identity-with-account-links/) [for more networks](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed/issues/1990)
* [Encrypt private keys in the datastore](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed/issues/794).

...and bug fixes:

* [Images not displaying on the Atmosphere (Bluesky)](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed/issues/2516)
* [HTML showing up in content warnings](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed/issues/2509)
* [Web login issues with Mastodon and Atmosphere](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed/issues/2506)
* [Fix Atmosphere login for PDSes that require appview proxying](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed/issues/2519), eg Tranquil
* [Update docs for Mastodon's web site verification](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed/issues/2505)
* Fix misc [invalid website](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed/issues/2478) [errors](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed/issues/2486)
* [Make unfollows more reliable](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed/issues/1631)

As usual, feel free to ping us with feedback, questions, and [bug reports](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed/issues), and [please support us on Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/c/ANewSocial)! You can follow the [_now_ label on GitHub](https://github.com/snarfed/bridgy-fed/issues?q=is%3Aopen+is%3Aissue+label%3Anow) to see what we're currently focusing on. See you on the bridge!
