<p>A glimpse into the <a href="https://fed.brid.gy/">Bridgy Fed</a> monitoring dashboards. Pretty conventional mix of infra, OS, and app level metrics.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Note the delay numbers. When you do something in one network, how quickly do we bridge it across? We pay a lot of attention to that, we try hard to keep it as fast as possible!</p>
